# MAM Quick Start Guide (End User – Contacts Enabled)

## Objective
Access company email, Teams and files securely on your personal device without enrolling it, with optional contact sync.

---

## 1. Install Company Portal

- Open App Store / Google Play
- Install: Company Portal

---

## 2. Sign in

- Open Company Portal
- Sign in with your work account

---

## 3. Install apps

From Company Portal install:
- Microsoft Outlook
- Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft OneDrive
- Microsoft Authenticator

---

## 4. Open Outlook

- Sign in with your work account
- Follow prompts

You will be asked to:
- Set a PIN
- Allow permissions

---

## 5. Enable Contacts Sync (IMPORTANT)

If enabled by your organization:

1. Allow Contacts permission when prompted
2. In Outlook settings ensure:
   - Save Contacts is enabled

---

## 6. Result

- Your Outlook contacts will appear in your phone contacts

Important behavior:
- iPhone: contacts are copied to your device
- Android: contacts availability depends on device behavior

---

## 7. Start working

You can now use:
- Email (Outlook)
- Teams
- Files (OneDrive)

Your device is NOT enrolled.
Only company data inside apps is managed and protected.
