# iOS Device Enrollment Guide (Company Portal)

## Objective

Set up your iPhone or iPad for work access using Company Portal and install required apps automatically.

---

## 1. Install Company Portal

- Open App Store
- Install: Company Portal

---

## 2. Sign in

- Open Company Portal
- Sign in with your work account

---

## 3. Start enrollment

- Tap:
  Begin / Enroll device
- Follow instructions

---

## 4. Install the management profile

When your organization enrolls your device, Company Portal will prompt you to install a management profile.

Follow these steps:

1. If you see a message saying **Profile Downloaded**, tap **Close**.
2. Open the **Settings** app.
3. Go to **Profile Downloaded** at the top of Settings.
   - If you do not see **Profile Downloaded**, go to **General → VPN & Device Management**.
4. Tap the downloaded management profile.
5. Tap **Install**.
6. Enter your device passcode if prompted.
7. Review the **Remote Management** warning and tap **Install**.
8. Tap **Trust** when prompted.
9. Tap **Done**.
10. Return to **Company Portal** to finish enrollment.

---

## 5. Complete enrollment

- Return to Company Portal
- Wait for enrollment to complete

---

## 6. Apps installation

After enrollment:

- The following apps will automatically install:
  - Outlook
  - Teams
  - OneDrive
  - Authenticator

---

## 7. Open Outlook

- Sign in with your work account
- Follow prompts

You will be asked to:
- Set a PIN
- Allow permissions (IMPORTANT: Contacts)

---

## 8. Contacts Sync (IMPORTANT)

If enabled by your organization:

1. Allow Contacts permission when prompted
2. Outlook will save your work contacts to your device

---

## 9. How contacts work on iPhone

### Contacts availability

- Your work contacts appear in:
  - iPhone Contacts app
  - Phone dialer
  - Messaging apps

---

### Caller identification

- Incoming calls will display names from your work contacts

---

### Important behavior

- Contacts are copied to your device  
- They are accessible by all apps on your phone  
- They are no longer fully protected by your organization  

---

## 10. Start working

You can now use:

- Outlook (email and calendar)  
- Teams (chat and meetings)  
- OneDrive (files)  

---

## Important Notes

✅ Your personal data remains unchanged  
✅ Your organization can manage work data and apps  
⚠️ Contacts (if enabled) are stored on your device
